Spanish Headstart for Latin America · Module 1 · Getting to know you

Unit 1 · Greetings and introductions

Saludos y presentación

Work through it in this order

  1. Conversation: listen, then shadow each line.
  2. Exercises 1–13: repeat, respond to cues, and say sentences in Spanish.
  3. Mixed practice: every cue from the unit, weakest first, out of order.
  4. Self-evaluation quiz: nine situations with no hints.

By the end of this unit you can

  • Greet people by time of day
  • Ask and answer "How are you?"
  • Introduce yourself and others
  • Respond to an introduction

This course was written for U.S. service members in Panama, so the examples use military ranks. The greetings and introductions are everyday Spanish.
